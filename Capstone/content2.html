Flower

Flowers are the reproductive structures of flowering plants (angiosperms), designed to produce seeds through pollination. They consist of essential parts like petals (to attract pollinators), stamens (male, produce pollen), and pistils (female, receive pollen). Flowers serve crucial ecological roles, providing food for insects/birds and adding aesthetic, cultural, or medicinal value to human life.

Flower (rose)

Roses (genus Rosa) are woody perennial flowering shrubs in the family Rosaceae, with over 300 species and thousands of cultivated varieties (cultivars). They are native to temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere, primarily Asia, with some species native to Europe, North America, and northwest Africa.

Flower (Frangipani)

Frangipani (Plumeria) flowers are renowned for their vibrant, waxy, five-petaled blooms that appear in clusters, often with a distinct, sweet fragrance that intensifies at night. While the classic variety is white with a yellow center, they come in a vast array of colors ranging from deep crimson, bright pink, and yellow to shades of orange, lilac, and multi-colored combinations.
